# MVP Scope

Phase 3 output. Coordinated for the 3-person manual team and 24-hour sprint (`2026-11-29` target). Complements core PS-02 rules without replacing them.

| Feature | Priority (P0/P1/P2/P3) | Acceptance criteria (P0 mandatory) | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication & seeded demo accounts | P0 | Email/password signup and login with `bcrypt` + JWT in `HttpOnly` cookie; `/api/v1/auth/me` returns user, employee link, role seat, assigned HR teams, and clearance; seeded `Internal` and `Confidential` demo accounts work out of the box (using synthetic demo records, never bundling participant `.xlsx`). | Role A (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Company setup & PostgreSQL/Prisma schema | P0 | Admin creates a company; Prisma schema models PS-02 tables (`Employee`, `WorkItem`, `Dependency`, `Decision`, `WorkbookRule`) plus app-layer tables (`User`, `Company`, `OnboardingRequest`, `RoleSeat`, `HrTeamAssignment`, `WorkExecutionProgress`, `ImpactNotification`). | Role C (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| MyGate-style manager-approval onboarding | P0 | New user submits join request (`Pending` blocks workspace data); manager/admin approves or rejects, linking/creating `Employee` and assigning clearance. | Role A + Role B (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Schema-validated `.xlsx` workbook importer | P0 | Admin uploads a 5-sheet `.xlsx` matching `PS02_Ripple_Effect` schema; Zod validates columns, enums, and FKs; replaces/loads company dataset without hardcoded sample IDs and without fabricating missing HR/progress history. | Role C (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Deterministic R1-R5 ripple engine & R4 redaction | P0 | Full-graph cycle-safe traversal (`prerequisite_work_id -> dependent_work_id`, including root); Drafts trigger zero actions (R1); `Closed` nodes traversed without notifying closed-item owner for that item (R3); distinct open owners notified; `Confidential` decisions redacted on server for `Internal` users with safe action context verified by automated tests (R4); `Reverse` applies `Withdrawn_Review` over same scope (R5). Assignment scoping never cuts off traversal. | Role A (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Explicit HR team assignments & role-bound successor handoff | P0 | Admin assigns specific teams to an HR role seat; HR sees only assigned teams (not downward hierarchy); transferring the HR role seat to a successor user inherits role-bound decision history & follow-through for those teams while keeping original `decider_id` immutable and enforcing R4 clearance of the active viewer. | Role A + Role B (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Team Lead execution progress (separate from Decision approval) | P0 | Team Lead updates execution progress (`Not_Started`, `In_Progress`, `Blocked`, `Completed` + note) on their team's affected work items without modifying `WorkItem.status` (`Open`/`Closed`) or conflating `Decision.status = Approved` with completion; HR tracks execution progress across assigned teams. | Role A + Role B (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Scoped workspace & PS-02 Ripple Explorer | P0 | Workspace views (`My Team`, `My Work`, `My Decisions`, `HR Assigned Teams`, `PS-02 Ripple Solver`) + interactive React Flow (`@xyflow/react`) graph showing root, hops, `Closed` pass-throughs, execution progress badges, and R4-redacted badges. | Role B (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Live decision authoring & state transitions | P0 | Authorized user creates `Draft` decision (no ripple notifications), approves a `Draft` (triggers R1-R4 notifications and initializes follow-through tracking), or creates/approves a `Reverse` decision (R5). | Role A + Role B (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Audit log (approvals, HR handoffs & decision history) | P1 | Chronological log of onboarding approvals, HR seat transfers, workbook imports, decision approvals, and reversals per company. | Role A + Role C (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| Downloadable impact & execution report (CSV/JSON) | P1/P2 | Export clearance-filtered ripple analysis and team execution progress summary as CSV or JSON. | Role B (name: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |
| What-if / Draft preview simulation | P2 | Non-persisting preview showing predicted affected scope if a `Draft` decision were approved, clearly labeled as non-triggering per R1. | Role A + Role B (names: TODO - TEAM DECISION REQUIRED) |

Rule: attempt fewer features than feels comfortable. P0 bugs outrank P2/P3 features, always.
